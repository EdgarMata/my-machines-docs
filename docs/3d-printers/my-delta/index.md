# Welcome to MY-Delta 3D Printer Documentation

![MY-Delta](../../assets/images/delta/my-delta_01.webp){ width="1000" }

## Welcome to the official documentation for the MY-Delta 3D Printer.
Inspired by the incredible Open Source Doron Velta project, the MY-Delta brings the speed and precision of delta kinematics to the DIY and Maker community. Here you will find all the information you need to set up, calibrate, and troubleshoot your digital fabrication machine.

!!! warning "Work In Progress (WIP) - Active Development"
    **The MY-Delta is currently under active development.**
    
    While we are refining the fast delta kinematics and the rigid triangular enclosure, the 3D models, printed parts, and the exact Bill of Materials (BOM) are still being optimized for the best DIY assembly experience. 
    
    *Want to embrace the speed of delta printing and support our project?* Sourcing components independently during this phase carries the risk of dimension mismatches as the design evolves. To guarantee hardware compatibility and directly support our Open Source R&D, we highly recommend getting your verified components straight from the [MY-Machines Official Store](https://www.my-machines.com).
